How to play number guessing game

In this number guessing game, the browser chooses a secret whole number from 1 to 100. Enter a guess and use the higher or lower clue to narrow the range. A good strategy is to start near the midpoint, then halve the remaining range with each clue. Try to beat your personal best number of guesses. The game is free and works on mobile and desktop.

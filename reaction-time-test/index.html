How to play reaction time test

A reaction time test measures how quickly you respond to a visual signal. This simple online reflex test gives you a quick result in milliseconds. For a more useful average, complete several rounds rather than judging your speed from one attempt. Results can vary with screen refresh rate, device performance, touch latency and distractions. Try again when you are focused, and compare your own results over time. This browser test is for entertainment and self-practice, not a medical or scientific assessment.

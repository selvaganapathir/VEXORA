WHY QUICKPLAY LAB?

Fun should be one tap away.

QuickPlay Lab is a collection of free, lightweight browser games and mini tests. No app install, no account and no complicated setup. Open a challenge and start playing.

Built to work on phones, tablets and desktops. The games run directly in your browser and keep your personal bests on your device.

THE BUILD

Designed and developed as a mobile-first web project with semantic HTML, lean CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Search-friendly pages, accessible controls and measurable engagement are part of the build.

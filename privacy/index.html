THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Privacy

QuickPlay Lab is a collection of browser-based mini games. Game scores and preferences are stored locally in your browser using local storage. They are not sent to a game account or leaderboard.

Analytics

If the site owner enables Google Analytics 4, Google may collect usage information such as page views, device and browser details, approximate location derived from IP address, and interactions. Analytics is disabled until the site owner adds a valid Measurement ID to analytics-config.js. See Google's privacy documentation for details.

Cookies and local storage

The games use browser local storage to remember personal best scores. Analytics cookies may be used only if Google Analytics is enabled by the site owner.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the website owner through the repository's public contact information.
